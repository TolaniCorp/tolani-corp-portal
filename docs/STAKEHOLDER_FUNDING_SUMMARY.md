# Stakeholder Funding Summary

## Purpose

This document gives funding stakeholders a short, portfolio-level view of where capital should go, what proof is needed, and what each platform is expected to become.

## Track 1: Operating Revenue

- Best fit: operators, family offices, strategic cashflow investors
- Focus platforms:
  - TCCG.work
  - BettorsACE
  - Tolani Supply Group
- Why now:
  - each can show near-term revenue if acquisition, support, and operations are disciplined
- What capital should fund:
  - growth systems with clear margin accountability
  - support automation and operator tooling
  - pipeline, retention, and account operations
- What diligence should test:
  - conversion and retention
  - gross margin profile
  - repeat customer or account behavior

## Track 2: Education and Workforce

- Best fit: grants, workforce boards, institutional partners, education-focused capital
- Focus platform:
  - Tolani Labs
- Why now:
  - Tolani Labs and DEBO can become a high-trust learning and credentialing system
- What capital should fund:
  - admissions and learner acquisition
  - curriculum and credential productization
  - DEBO workstation and management telemetry
- What diligence should test:
  - learner activation
  - completion and credential rates
  - partner demand and cohort economics

## Track 3: Strategic Partnerships

- Best fit: travel, logistics, communications, data, and channel partners
- Focus platforms:
  - Hook Travel
  - Tolani Supply Group
  - BettorsACE
- Why now:
  - distribution and service quality can improve faster through partners than pure paid growth
- What partnerships should fund or enable:
  - supplier relationships
  - communications infrastructure
  - channel distribution
  - data and operator leverage
- What diligence should test:
  - service-level compatibility
  - routing and escalation fit
  - joint conversion potential

## Track 4: Future Treasury

- Best fit: ecosystem-aligned, long-horizon supporters
- Focus platform:
  - TUT / Tolani Ecosystem DAO
- Why now:
  - only as a future layer after operating proof exists
- What capital should fund:
  - contract hardening
  - governance controls
  - reward design tied to real product telemetry
- What diligence should test:
  - auditability
  - utility clarity
  - linkage to profitable product behavior

## Stakeholder Summary by Platform

### Tolani Labs

- Funding posture: strongest strategic growth story
- Capital shape: education capital, workforce grants, institutional partnerships
- Proof to emphasize: student outcomes and DEBO usage

### TCCG.work

- Funding posture: cashflow and services discipline
- Capital shape: working capital, strategic debt, project finance
- Proof to emphasize: margins, backlog quality, win rate

### BettorsACE

- Funding posture: consumer recurring revenue
- Capital shape: retention-led growth capital
- Proof to emphasize: premium conversion, retention, trust-backed performance

### Hook Travel

- Funding posture: service brand with leverage through partners
- Capital shape: strategic travel and operations capital
- Proof to emphasize: booking rate, repeat behavior, recovery quality

### Listo Marketplace

- Funding posture: early and proof-sensitive
- Capital shape: patient seed or strategic commerce support
- Proof to emphasize: merchant quality, dispute rate, recurring merchant revenue

### Tolani Supply Group

- Funding posture: B2B control-tower build
- Capital shape: strategic partner capital and working-capital support
- Proof to emphasize: retained accounts, turnaround discipline, margin per account

### TUT / DAO

- Funding posture: delayed until operating proof exists
- Capital shape: limited treasury and ecosystem capital
- Proof to emphasize: real utility and audit-ready controls
