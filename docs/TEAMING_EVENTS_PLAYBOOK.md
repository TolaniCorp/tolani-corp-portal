# Teaming Events Playbook

## Principle

Teaming events should create movement, not just visibility.

- Each event needs a target stakeholder lane.
- Each event needs a proof pack.
- Each event needs a named follow-up owner.
- Each event should route leads into the right system within 24 hours.

## Event Formats

### Tolani Portfolio Demo Day

- Goal: explain the portfolio as one operating system
- Audience: strategic partners, investors, operators
- Required assets:
  - strategy summary
  - live command surfaces
  - per-platform proof blocks
- Success criteria:
  - meetings scheduled
  - partner interest tagged by platform
  - follow-up routed into HQ communications

### DEBO Student Workstation Showcase

- Goal: convert education interest into cohort and partner conversations
- Audience: schools, workforce partners, mentors
- Required assets:
  - student journey demo
  - DEBO workstation view
  - cohort and credential plan
- Success criteria:
  - partner follow-up scheduled
  - admissions pipeline growth
  - DEBO adoption interest validated

### BettorsACE War Room Preview

- Goal: convert premium prospects through live product experience
- Audience: high-intent subscribers and affiliates
- Required assets:
  - War Room demo
  - settlement-backed proof
  - support readiness narrative
- Success criteria:
  - trial-to-paid movement
  - premium objections captured
  - War Room conversion tracked

### Hook Travel Concierge Partner Table

- Goal: secure supplier and support relationships
- Audience: travel suppliers, military networks, service partners
- Required assets:
  - concierge flow
  - service-recovery SOP
  - partner onboarding brief
- Success criteria:
  - partner introductions advanced
  - approved partner list created
  - service quality lift measured

### Trade Control Tower Briefing

- Goal: position TSG as a retained operating partner
- Audience: importers, exporters, freight and customs allies
- Required assets:
  - intake workflow
  - control-tower narrative
  - retainer offer sheet
- Success criteria:
  - retained-account discussions opened
  - high-fit accounts routed into pipeline
  - turnaround standards enforced

## Event Operations Checklist

- Confirm audience and target lane.
- Confirm live demos and proof are accurate.
- Prepare operator routing before the event starts.
- Define CRM tags and follow-up owners.
- Record outcomes and objections.
- Send stakeholder-specific follow-up within one business day.

## Event Artifacts to Keep in Repo

- one-pager for each platform
- funding summary by stakeholder type
- operator and communications routing notes
- post-event debrief with conversion outcomes

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not oversell features that are not live.
- Do not use one generic follow-up deck for every stakeholder type.
- Do not let event leads die in email without system routing.
- Do not treat token or treasury narrative as the lead story ahead of operating proof.
