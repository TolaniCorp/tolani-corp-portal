HR & Contractor Workflow

Pre-Onboarding

  • Contractor Agreement: Signed via Deel.
  • NDA: Signed legal document.
  • IP Assignment: Full transfer of work product.
  • Tax Forms: W-9 / W-8BEN compliance.
  • ID Verification: Copy of valid identification.

Day 1: System Access

  • Email: Corporate account setup.
  • Slack: Join ecosystem workspace.
  • GitHub: Add to organization.
  • Linear/Notion: Project management tools.
  • Deel: Payment portal access.

Week 1 Checkpoints

  • Welcome Session: Mission & Culture overview.
  • Handbook Review: OpSec & Comms norms.
  • Project Kickoff: Scope & Deliverables.
  • Initial Task: First commit or deliverable.
  • Manager Sync: First week progress check.
