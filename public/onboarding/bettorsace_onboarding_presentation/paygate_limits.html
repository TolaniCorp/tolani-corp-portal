Paygate Limits & Expectations

Free Tier

Low-volume evaluation path. Hard ceilings on requests and data access. Designed for initial discovery.

Pro Tier

Paid path for active integrations and SMB usage. Higher throughput with standard commercial terms.

Enterprise

High-volume, contract-bound access. Controlled onboarding with custom limits and dedicated support.

Team Behavior Expectations

Do not bypass quota or rate controls in ad hoc code or scripts.

Do not grant enterprise-level tool access without a formal approval path.
