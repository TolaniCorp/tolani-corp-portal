30/60/90 Day Ramp Plan

30
  • Learn architecture, policies, and playbooks
  • Shadow support and incident triage
  • Pass basic security knowledge check
60
  • Own one reliability and support workflow
  • Deliver one documented process improvement
  • Participate in live incident response
90
  • Run incident lead rotation for simulated event
  • Present hardening recommendations
  • Full certification for production access

Ramp outcomes are measurable: fewer escalations, faster mitigation, cleaner handoffs.
