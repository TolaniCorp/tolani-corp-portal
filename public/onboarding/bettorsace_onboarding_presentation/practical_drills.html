Practical Drills (Required)

Drill 1: Paygate Validation

Execute tests to confirm correct tier behavior for Free, Pro, and Enterprise accounts across core API endpoints.

Drill 2: Secret Leak & Rotation

Participate in a tabletop exercise for secret exposure and execute a live rotation of high-risk credentials.

Drill 3: Incident Rollback

Simulate a production incident and perform a rollback to a last known-good deployment while monitoring health.

Drill 4: Auth Failure Triage

Diagnose and resolve common authentication failures, including callback and audience mismatches.

Teams retain runbooks through practical drills, not static slides. Certification requires hands-on demonstration.
