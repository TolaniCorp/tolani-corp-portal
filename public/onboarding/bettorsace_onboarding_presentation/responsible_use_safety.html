Responsible Use & Safety

Gambling Controls

Enforce responsible gambling limits and cooling-off periods through backend logic, not just UI toggles.

No Guarantees

Never frame system outputs or AI picks as "guaranteed wins." Maintain a stance of statistical probability.

Risk Commentary

Keep risk assessments aligned with model confidence and explicitly state underlying uncertainty.

Immediate Escalation

Report policy-violating content or suspicious user behavior immediately to the safety lead.

Safety is not an add-on; it is a core component of product quality and our legal posture.
