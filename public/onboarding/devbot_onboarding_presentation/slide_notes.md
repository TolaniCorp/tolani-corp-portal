Our solution is DevBot, an autonomous AI engineer that integrates directly into your tools, writes code, and ships PRs, freeing developers to focus on higher-value tasks. This vision drives every decision we make, from architecture to product strategy. Now, let's look at how DevBot fits into our broader ecosystem.

DevBot is the core of our ecosystem, but it's part of a larger, interconnected system we call DevTown. Let's dive into the technical foundation that makes this possible.

# 4 - Platform Architecture

Our platform architecture is built for performance, intelligence, and seamless integration. We use TypeScript and Node.js for a type-safe, high-performance core runtime, ensuring efficient execution of autonomous tasks. The intelligence engine, powered by Claude AI, provides advanced reasoning, code synthesis, and sophisticated task planning. Convex and Postgres form our data foundation, enabling real-time state management and persistent agent memory. Finally, our interface layer integrates with Slack, Discord, and GitHub, providing a native developer experience. This robust stack is the backbone of our autonomous engineering capabilities. Now, let's see how this architecture translates into our product strategy and roadmap.

# 5 - Product Strategy & Roadmap

Our product strategy is focused on delivering increasing value and scaling our impact. We launched our MVP in Q1 2026 with a Slack bot and GitHub integration, quickly moving to a public beta in Q2 with a customer dashboard and Stripe integration. Q3 will see us scale with Linear and Jira integrations and achieve SOC 2 audit compliance. By Q4, we'll offer enterprise solutions, including white-label and self-hosted options. Our North Star metric is 'Hours Saved' per customer per month, and we're targeting less than 5% churn to prove product-market fit. This roadmap guides our development and ensures we're always moving towards our vision. Next, we'll discuss the critical operational and security aspects that underpin everything we do.

# 6 - Operations & Security

Operations and security are not afterthoughts; they are foundational to our product and our mission. We embed these principles into every stage of our development lifecycle. Our release guardrails ensure that every deployment is robust and reversible. Automated testing and mandatory peer reviews catch issues early, while strict secret hygiene prevents critical vulnerabilities. This proactive approach minimizes risk and maintains our operational integrity. Our incident response protocols are equally rigorous. We prioritize immediate action for critical issues, using NATT agents for targeted scanning and immutable audit logs for every action. This allows us to quickly identify, mitigate, and learn from any incident, including mandatory secret rotation after any suspected breach. This commitment to security and operational excellence is what allows us to move fast and build with confidence.
