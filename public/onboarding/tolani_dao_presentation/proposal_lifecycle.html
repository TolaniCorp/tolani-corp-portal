From Idea to Execution

01

Drafting & Discussion

Community members draft proposals and discuss them in the official forum to gather feedback and refine the scope.

Community Consensus
02

Snapshot Signaling

A gasless vote on Snapshot gauges community sentiment. This acts as a non-binding signal before moving to on-chain action.

Soft Quorum Required
03

Formal On-Chain Proposal

The proposal is formally submitted to the Tolani Governor contract. This initiates the official voting period for TUT holders.

TUT Delegation Required
04

Voting & Timelock

If quorum is reached and the vote passes, the proposal enters the Timelock for a 48-hour security delay before execution.

48H Security Delay
05

On-Chain Execution

The approved action is executed by the Gnosis Safe multi-sig, completing the lifecycle and implementing the community's decision across the ecosystem.

Final Execution
