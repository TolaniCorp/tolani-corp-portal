Legal Foundation

The Tolani Ecosystem DAO operates as a registered Wyoming DAO LLC, providing a robust legal framework that bridges decentralized governance with traditional corporate compliance.

This structure ensures that community decisions are legally recognized while maintaining the transparency and security of the blockchain.
