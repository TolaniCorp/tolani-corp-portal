Wyoming Compliance

The Tolani Ecosystem DAO adheres to the Wyoming Decentralized Autonomous Organization LLC Act, ensuring legal recognition of community-led governance.

Our Attachment C Compliance Addendum provides clear guidelines for DAO operations, risk posture, and member protections in a regulated environment.

Risk Management

  • Treasury Monitoring: Real-time tracking of DAO-owned assets and liquidity positions.
  • Protocol Audits: Continuous security reviews of governance and treasury smart contracts.
  • Governance Guardrails: Timelock mechanisms and quorum requirements to prevent malicious actions.
  • Transparent Reporting: Publicly accessible audit trails for every proposal and execution.
